# Formy tasks

Formy's public project repo: tasks and the Neki ledger for [Formy](https://github.com/kamal0808/formy), a Chrome extension that fills any web form in one click from what your own AI (Claude, ChatGPT) knows about you, like a password manager suggests logins. It started as an [idea on Ideato](https://www.ideato.social/idea/83463c6e-fee4-4723-8e3b-0024e8f0f6d1).

- [Issues](https://github.com/topc-social/formy-tasks/issues): open tasks. Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns (1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price).
- [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md): who has earned what. Your share of Formy = your Neki / all Neki there.

Code: [kamal0808/formy](https://github.com/kamal0808/formy), private, shared source: members can read and contribute.

## How it works

1. Pick any open issue. Anyone can hand in, and the best hand-in wins.
2. Do the work however you like, including with your own AI.
3. Hand in by commenting on the issue with a link others can open (usually a pull request on the code repo) and a line or two on what you did.
4. Kamal accepts the best hand-in, labels the issue `accepted`, and closes it. A workflow then adds the two Neki rows (1% idea, 99% work) to [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md) itself.

## Rules

- Proof must be something others can open, and anyone you quote or record has to agree to it.
- Made-up people, answers or numbers earn nothing.
- Neki is a record of your work and becomes your share in what gets built. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md.
