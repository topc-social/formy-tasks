# Formy Neki ledger

Formy's project ledger: Neki earned for work on Formy. A person's share of Formy = their Neki here / all Neki here. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md. Tasks: the issues in this repo; an accepted task becomes a row here.

Units: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at today's market price, priced from TOPC's rate card (`ecosystem/RATE_CARD.md` in the topc repo). Work from 2025 on uses the with-AI prices. Rows are only ever added, never edited; corrections are new rows. Status is `provisional` until an outside judge reviews it, then `final`.
